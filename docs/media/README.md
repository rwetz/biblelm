# BibleLM screenshots and walkthrough

Screenshots captured 1 November 2026 at 1280 × 720. The walkthrough was
re-recorded 2 November 2026 at native 3840 × 2160 from the running browser app.
These show the current working tree, including unreleased work,
rather than the v2.1.0 installer. No invented UI, personal notes or live keys.

The walkthrough shows Home (0–4s), Read (4–9s), the verse panel (9–13s),
Hebrew word study (13–18s), Gallery (18–22s), a painting with its passage
(22–30s). It records actual browser frames in a 3840 × 2160 viewport, with
the interface's Auto scale at 150%. The capture contains 48 frames over
30 seconds, approximately 1.6 captures a second, preserved at their capture
times in a 30 fps encode. Transitions are sampled rather than captured at
native 30 fps. No upscaling or monitor-setting changes were used. Both encodes
were checked for dimensions and duration, and an encoded painting frame was
visually inspected. The still screenshots and website poster remain at 720p.

Public-domain artwork and Scripture sources remain credited in the UI;
see [art credits](../ART.md) and [data provenance](../DATA.md).

Website exports are in the personal website's `src/assets/showcase/`:
`biblelm.webp` is the poster; `biblelm.mp4` and `biblelm.webm` are the video;
`biblelm-<screen>.webp` are the seven screenshots. Existing `biblelm.png` was
preserved. The website's ongoing redesign can use these without changing its
layout here. Use a static poster under reduced motion and pause hidden video.
