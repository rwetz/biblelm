# Polish and capture review, 1 November 2026

The roadmap and the previous end-to-end review were checked first. Their
repo-local milestones are complete. This pass fixes current defects rather
than reviving dropped hosted commentary or the separate Axon release work.

## Fixed

- Loading a different translation no longer exposes the previous edition's
  text under the new selection. A regression checks the first render and an
  old request completing after a newer one.
- Clipboard actions await success, handle rejection and an unavailable API,
  and credit borrowed passages to their actual source. A missing verse's
  copy button is disabled.
- The rose mark exits safely if its 2D context is unavailable. Smoke tests
  exercise this case and reject the page's crash screen.
- The Gallery subtitle is below the hero. Engravings are sorted in Bible
  order; detail crops are excluded from both the collection and its count.
- At 900 × 600, the floating verse panel starts below the toolbar. Translation,
  chapter and parallel controls stay visible. The body still sits behind the
  panel, which is the intended narrow-window treatment.
- Theme transition snapshots ignore pointer input. Settings now describes
  the query emblem rather than the obsolete odds and timing display.

Two issues belong to the pinned `@nexis/design` package and remain open:

- Its collapsed `SidebarNav` removes visible labels without adding accessible
  button names. The full sidebar is labelled, and the command palette remains
  available; the icon rail needs an upstream `aria-label` fix.

  transitions. The browser logged `AbortError: Transition was skipped. New

  does not resolve the shared provider's unhandled transition promises.
